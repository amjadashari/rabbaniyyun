**Dr. Mohamad Khir Bin Johari, MB BCh, MSc**, is a **PhD Candidate in Cardiovascular Sciences** at the University of Manchester. He previously completed his Master of Science in Interventional Cardiovascular Medicine at the University of Galway and his medical training at Ain Shams University. His diverse academic background also includes degrees in Shariah and Da'wah Islamiyyah from the Islamic University of Madinah and Al-Azhar University.
